# CCTV stock audio

These sound effects were downloaded from Mixkit and are used under the
[Mixkit Sound Effects Free License](https://mixkit.co/license/#sfxFree).
Attribution is not required, but source information is retained here.

| Local file | Mixkit title | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `remote-button-click.mp3` | On or off light switch tap | https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/click/ |
| `tv-static-loop.mp3` | Long static noise | https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/static/ |
| `channel-static.mp3` | Radio static fx | https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/static/ |
| `teletext-tone.mp3` | Digital quick tone | https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/tones/ |

The files retain their downloaded MP3 encoding. Playback levels and looping
are intentionally controlled in `js/app.js` through `cctvSoundLibrary`.
